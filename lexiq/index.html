How to Play LexIQ

🎯 Goal

Guess the hidden 5-letter word in 6 attempts or fewer.

⌨️ How to Play

  • Type a valid 5-letter word using your physical keyboard or the on-screen keyboard.
  • Press Enter to submit your guess.

🎨 Tile Colors

After each guess, the tile colors reveal how close you are:

GREEN Correct position: Letter is in the word and in the right spot.
YELLOW Wrong position: Letter is in the word, but in a different spot.
GRAY Not in word: Letter does not appear anywhere in the hidden word.

🔍 Example

Suppose the hidden word is PLANT and your guess is PLAYS:

P
L
A
Y
S

🟩 P, L, A are green — in the word and in the exact correct spots.

⬜ Y is gray — not present in the hidden word at all.

🟨 S is yellow — present in the word, but in the wrong spot.

💡 Hints & Scoring

Smart Hints (Max 2)

  • Hint 1: Reveals one correct letter and its position.
  • Hint 2: Gives a helpful clue about the word's meaning.
  • Penalty: Each hint reduces your score by 15 points.

Scoring & Streak

  • Fewer Attempts = Higher Score: Attempt 1 = 100 pts, down to Attempt 6 = 50 pts (0 on loss).
  • Streak: Consecutive wins build your Current Streak. A loss resets it to 0.

About LexIQ

LexIQ is an original, lightweight word guessing puzzle game designed for a college AI Tools Course Mini-Project.

Technology Stack

  • HTML5: Semantic elements, ARIA accessibility, responsive viewports.
  • CSS3: Custom theme variables, flex/grid layouts, smooth animations, accessible contrast.
  • Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+): Two-pass letter validation logic, state machine, local storage persistence.
  • Web Audio API: Pure algorithmic audio synthesizer for native, zero-dependency sound effects.

AI-Assisted Development

This project was developed with the assistance of modern generative AI coding tools for ideation, UI styling suggestions, duplicate letter matching algorithms, and automated test design.
