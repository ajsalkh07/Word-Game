How the Games Work

Quick rules for every title in the SYNAPSE library.

LexIQ Rules

Word Puzzle Strategy

  1. Objective: Guess the hidden 5-letter word in 6 attempts or fewer.
  2. Tile Colors: Green = correct spot, Yellow = wrong spot, Gray = not in word.
  3. Hints: Up to 2 clues available per game (each hint reduces final score).
  4. Score: Fewer attempts grant higher scores and build your win streak.

TEN Rules

10-Second Reflex Blitz

  1. 10 Seconds: Exactly 10.0 seconds per round across 10 randomized challenges.
  2. Speed Matters: Faster responses reward huge Speed Bonus multipliers.
  3. Combos: Consecutive correct answers build combos; mistakes break them.
  4. 8 Modes: Math, Odd One Out, Shape Counting, Patterns, Memory, Sorting, Targets, and Reaction.
