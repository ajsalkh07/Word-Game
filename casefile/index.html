Examine the crime scene, interrogate the suspects, uncover the timeline, and find the contradiction to identify the culprit.

MOTIVE

Greed. Attempted to steal the antique solid-gold trophy to sell to a private collector off-campus.

METHOD

Used a fabricated light repair work order to access the corridor. Pried the desk drawer for the cabinet key, killed the camera breaker, and concealed the disassembled trophy inside his heavy toolbox.

THE CRITICAL CONTRADICTION

Ryan claimed he never stepped inside the trophy room and only repaired an exterior corridor light. However:
• The facilities work-boot tread impression in the room wax matched his exact footwear.
• Industrial cart grease from his toolbox was smeared on the pried key drawer and cabinet lock.
• The circuit breaker was unlocked and manually flipped with a service key at 4:17 PM, disproving his claim of an accidental bulb power surge.
